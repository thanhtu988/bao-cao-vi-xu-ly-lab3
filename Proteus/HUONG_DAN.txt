LAB 3 - MACH PROTEUS (lab3.pdsprj)
==================================
Linh kien: STM32F103C6 + 12 den (PA1-PA12) + 4 LED 7 doan chung anode qua PNP
           (SEG0-6 = PB0-PB6, EN0-3 = PB8-PB11) + 3 nut nhan BUTTON1-3 (PB12-PB14)
           co dien tro keo len 10k (R17-R19).
Program File cua STM32 tro toi ..\Lab3\Debug\Lab3.hex

CHAY: mo Proteus bang "Run as administrator" -> Open Project -> lab3.pdsprj -> Play

TEST
  Mode 1: den chay DO 5s -> XANH 3s -> VANG 2s; tuyen 1 bat dau DO 05, tuyen 2 bat dau XANH 03
  Nut 1 (BUTTON1): mode 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 1
  Mode 2/3/4: 4 den DO/VANG/XANH nhap nhay 2 Hz (doi trang thai moi 250 ms),
              LED tuyen 1 hien gia tri, LED tuyen 2 hien 02/03/04
  Nut 2 (BUTTON2): tang gia tri, 99 -> 01
  Nut 3 (BUTTON3): luu gia tri; chua bam nut 3 ma doi mode thi gia tri vua chinh bi bo

Luu y: Proteus mo phong STM32 cham hon thoi gian thuc (xem CPU load o thanh trang thai),
nen tren may tinh den co the nhap nhay cham hon 2 Hz; tren kit that thoi gian dung.
